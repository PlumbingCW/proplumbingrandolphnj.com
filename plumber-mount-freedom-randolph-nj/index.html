Mount Freedom is an unincorporated community inside Randolph Township, centered on Sussex Turnpike near West Hanover Avenue and Church Road. It is a mix of older houses, newer townhouses and apartment buildings, and that mix changes who you call first when the water is on the floor.

This page is for Mount Freedom residents and property managers who want a plumber to call. We take calls by phone only, and a licensed plumber is arranged for your address.

Housing mix

A village center with several kinds of homes

The township's land use plan describes a village center along Sussex Turnpike between Millbrook Avenue and West Hanover Avenue, with zoning that allows townhouses, villas, single-family, duplex or triplex housing. It also names multifamily communities in the area, including Woodmont and Brookside Village Apartments, plus townhouses on West Hanover Avenue.

The housing element lists Woodmont and Brookside Village as affordable housing programs from the 1990s. The takeaway is simple. Some Mount Freedom homes are owned houses and others are condos or rentals where an association or landlord controls part of the plumbing.

Tenants should not hire a plumber without telling the landlord. Many leases make the owner responsible for pipes and heaters, and a surprise repair bill can become a dispute. Owners and managers are welcome to call us for a leak survey when the source is unclear.

Sewer

Sewer service along Sussex Turnpike

The township's utilities plan puts the southern half of Randolph, along Sussex Turnpike, in the Butterworth sewer service area, with the treatment plant owned by Morris Township. The same plan notes that a 116-unit project approved in the Mount Freedom neighborhood had not started because of sewer moratorium issues with Morris Township.

For a homeowner, the important point is that a backup may be in your line or farther down. A camera look finds out which. Our Morris Township page covers the neighboring town that owns the plant, and our drain cleaning page explains the first step for a slow drain.

History

From summer resort to year-round community

The Mount Freedom Presbyterian Church, at Sussex Turnpike and Church Road, dates to 1826 on the township landmark map. A county history article says that at its peak Randolph had more than 50 hotels and bungalow colonies in the south of the township, and reference works describe Mount Freedom as a summer resort for much of the twentieth century.

Buildings that began as seasonal lodging can have been converted and added to over the decades. A plumber can show you which pipes are original and which were added later. Do not assume anything about the material until someone looks.
