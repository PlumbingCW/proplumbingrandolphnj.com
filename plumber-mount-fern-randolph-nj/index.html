Mount Fern is the name the township uses for a residential neighborhood around Quaker Church Road and Dover-Chester Road in Randolph. If you live there and need a plumber, this page explains what we do and what to check first. The address you use is Randolph, ZIP 07869.

We take calls by phone and arrange a licensed plumber for the address. We do not list a drawn boundary for Mount Fern, because we did not find one.

The area

How we place Mount Fern

The township's landmark map lists the Mount Fern United Methodist Church, built in 1883, at the corner of Quaker Church Road and Dover-Chester Road. It also lists the Moses Sayre Sr. House at 124 Dover-Chester Road, from about 1813, and the Daniel Lawrence Farmhouse at 180 Dover-Chester Road, from about 1796.

Those are our anchors. We use them to describe where Mount Fern is without claiming a boundary. If your address is close, call and ask. The plumbing answer is the same across Randolph.

Housing

Single-family streets and a small modern subdivision

An older section of the township master plan calls Mount Fern a neighborhood and groups it with the sewered single-family areas, along with Shongum Lake and Ironia. It also mentions a seven-lot subdivision named Heritage Village 17, completed in the neighborhood when that plan was written.

Newer and older houses are side by side. A plumber should look at the house in front of them, since neighbors can have different pipes, different heaters and different drains. Ask us about water heater installation if you are planning a change.

Everyday problems

Calls that start small

A running toilet is the most common cause of a high water bill, according to the township's page. A dye test in the tank shows whether the flapper is leaking. If the color reaches the bowl in about twenty minutes, the flapper needs replacing.

If the toilet is fine and the bill is still high, ask the township to recheck the meter. If the meter is accurate, the township says you may need a plumber. Our toilet repair and leak detection pages cover both steps.

Nearby

Neighbors along the same roads

The Randolph Friends Meeting House on Quaker Church Road dates to 1758, and a county history ties the early Quaker settlement to the Randolph and Roxbury border area. Our Roxbury page covers that neighboring town. Closer to home, see Center Grove and Ironia.
