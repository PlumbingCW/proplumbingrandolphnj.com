Where the 24/7 line sends plumbers

The line covers Randolph Township (ZIP 07869, with Mount Freedom 07970 and Ironia 07845) and five nearby towns that have their own pages. Each page explains the local ZIP codes, the water and sewer arrangements and the kind of housing that tends to cause calls.

Towns with their own page

Dover and Denville sit closest along the Route 10 corridor. Roxbury, Rockaway Township and Morris Township round out the ring.

  • DoverOur closest northern neighbor, with a historic downtown and a housing stock that skews old.
  • DenvilleRiver-valley township with lake communities, township wells and a long list of low-lying basements.
  • RoxburySuccasunna, Ledgewood, Kenvil and Landing, four mailing names under one large township.
  • Rockaway TownshipA big lake-studded township where freeze-ups, thaw and summer use drive the calls.
  • Morris TownshipMail says Morristown or Convent Station, but the township is a true neighbor on Randolph's southeast side.

A note on ZIP codes and town names

ZIP codes and town borders rarely line up. Morris Township addresses read Morristown or Convent Station, and Rockaway Township addresses read Rockaway. When you call, give the street address and the name on your mail, and the dispatcher will match it to the right area.
