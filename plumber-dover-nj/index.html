If you need an emergency plumber in Dover, NJ, call the 24/7 line. A dispatcher takes your address and sends a licensed plumber from our base on Route 10 in Randolph, the town just south of Dover.

Dover is compact. Census counted 18,460 people on about 2.7 square miles of land, so streets are closely built and many buildings hold more than one household. That changes how we work. A clog in one unit can surface in the apartment below, and a leak on the second floor reaches a ceiling quickly, so we ask about neighbors on the first call.

Housing age

Dover's houses are old, and old houses raise old questions

Dover's 2025 Housing Element, built on Census ACS data from 2023, counts 6,621 housing units in town. Of those, 30.6 percent went up before 1940 and 44.8 percent before 1950. A house's age does not tell us what is in the wall, but it tells us where to look first when a pipe fails.

Dover on the map, just north of our Route 10 base

Downtown

Blackwell Street and the blocks around it

The Blackwell Street Historic District is on the National Register, and the local historical society counts 52 contributing buildings across about 25 acres. Many are 1800s commercial buildings with apartments or offices above a storefront. When a supply line lets go upstairs, the shop below takes the water, so shutting the water off quickly and tracing the source matters as much as the repair.

Owners and managers on blocks like these can read about commercial plumbing service for after-hours failures. Tenants should also call the building owner, since shutoffs are often shared between units.

Utilities

Town water, quarterly bills and the pipe into your house

Dover's water comes from the Dover Water Commission, which draws on three groundwater wells on Princeton Avenue. The same system also serves Victory Gardens and parts of Wharton, Randolph and Rockaway Township. The town mails one combined water and sewer bill each quarter, so a bill that jumps is your cue to check toilets first and then book a leak hunt.

The Commission's 2025 water report says its lead service line inventory is available through its office, and that it is responsible for removing lead pipes. We cannot tell you what lies under your street, so call the Commission to ask. If the trouble is on the supply pipe inside your property, we can discuss water line repair with you.

Flood zones

Mapped flood hazard areas near the Rockaway River

Dover's 2025 Housing Element places several redevelopment sites, including the NJ Transit yard, in a flood zone, and says one site on Bassett Highway sits next to the Rockaway River. An earlier land inventory counted 217.91 acres inside the FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area. If your basement is low, test the sump before the next storm and ask about a battery backup, since sump pump repair is a call we take at night too.

What to do before our plumber reaches your Dover address

These four steps keep damage down while the line connects you to a crew.

1Find the main valve

In older Dover houses it may sit in a basement corner or behind a panel. Turn it slowly. If it will not move, leave it alone and tell the dispatcher.

2Kill the power to wet areas

If a water heater is leaking, switch it off at the breaker. Stay away from outlets near standing water.

3Clear a path

Move boxes from under sinks and from the basement stairs. In a multi-unit building, warn the households below and your landlord.

4Smell gas? Go outside

Leave the building, move well away, and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Call us after, not before.

Neighbors

Dover, Victory Gardens and Mine Hill are three different places

Victory Gardens is its own borough, not a Dover neighborhood, even though its mail carries a Dover address. It was incorporated in 1951 from part of Randolph Township. If you live there, say so on the call, because water, permits and billing questions go to different offices.

Mine Hill is a separate township. Its own housing plan places Dover Town on its eastern edge, and permit questions there go to Mine Hill, not to Dover. Rockaway Township, which Dover's plan places to the north, has its own page at Rockaway Township, and Denville has its own page as well.

Permits

Ask the plumbing subcode office before a big job

Dover's construction department posts state UCC forms and a note on when a permit is not necessary. Its page does not mention water heaters, so we do not claim a rule either way. Call the Dover construction office before a replacement, and you can check any plumber on the state's License Verification System.
