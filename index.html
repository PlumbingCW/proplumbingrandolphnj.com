First ten minutes

What to do before the emergency plumber arrives

Most water damage happens in the first hour, so the steps below matter as much as the repair. Do them only if you can reach the spot safely and the floor around it is dry.

  1. 1

    Stop the water

    Close the main shut-off valve, usually where the line enters the basement or utility room. For a single fixture, use the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet.

  2. 2

    Protect the electrics

    If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, leave that area alone and switch off power at the breaker only if the breaker is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.

  3. 3

    Open the taps above the leak

    After the main is closed, opening a faucet on the top floor drains the pipes and lowers the pressure behind a burst line.

  4. 4

    Call the line and describe what you see

    Tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from, what has stopped working and whether anyone smells gas. Gas smell means leave first, then call from outside.

Not sure whether it counts as an emergency? Describe it to the dispatcher and get a straight answer.

Local

Randolph streets, parks and plumbing we know

Randolph is spread out. Route 10 carries most of the commercial traffic past County College of Morris at Center Grove Road, while Dover-Chester Road and Sussex Turnpike connect the quieter residential sections. Our base sits on Route 10, so a call from the Route 10 corridor starts close to the truck.

Homes near Brundage Park, Freedom Park and the Carrell Road area span several decades. About 54 percent of the township's 9,356 housing units were built before 1980, according to the township's housing plan, so aging supply lines, original water heaters and old drain lines are realistic suspects when something fails.

Older neighborhoods such as Shongum, Center Grove and Fernbrook, and the historic Combs Hollow and Gristmill Road areas, often have a mix of original and updated plumbing after decades of remodels. Ask the plumber to show you which sections are original before you approve a larger repair.

Mount Freedom (ZIP 07970) and Ironia (ZIP 07845) are Randolph communities too, and the main township ZIP is 07869. Whichever address you use, the same 24/7 line covers you.
