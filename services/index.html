Emergency or planned work

An emergency is water you cannot stop, sewage coming back up, no water at all, or a leak near electrics. Those jobs go to the front of the queue. A dripping faucet, a slow drain or a water heater that is old but still working can wait for a same-day or next-day appointment.

Planned work still needs the right paperwork. In Randolph, water heater and boiler replacement need a construction permit, and fixture or piping changes need a permit and zoning approval. The township's Office of Construction Codes is at 973-989-7070.

Unsure which bucket you are in? Call and ask.
