Roxbury Township is one municipality with several names on the mail. Residents say Succasunna, Ledgewood, Kenvil or Landing, and an emergency plumber in Roxbury, NJ needs to know all four. Call our 24/7 line, give the community and the street, and a licensed plumber is dispatched from our base in Randolph.

The 2020 Census counted 22,950 people here, and 88.2 percent of homes are owner-occupied. Owners usually decide on repairs themselves, so we describe the options and price the work before it begins.

Succasunna

Succasunna, the largest community and the primary ZIP

ZIP 07876 is Succasunna, the biggest of the township's mailing communities, which is why we list it first. Succasunna Brook is one of the two brook corridors the county flood plan names, with Drakes Brook. If your house sits near a brook, a failed sump pump or a floor drain that bubbles after heavy rain is a plumbing call we handle, and sump pump repair is a frequent request.

Ledgewood and Kenvil

Ledgewood, Kenvil and the water company question

Two water suppliers serve the township according to a 2025 Highlands Council draft report. New Jersey American Water covers the south and the Roxbury Township Water Department covers the north, and both draw from wells. The American Water company took over the Roxbury Water Company system, close to 4,000 accounts, at the end of 2018.

That split decides who answers a pressure complaint or a main break, so check your bill before calling. For a leak between the street and your house, our water line repair team can check the pipe on your side.

Overview

Round-the-clock dispatch across all of Roxbury

Our dispatch line never closes, so a Roxbury call at 3 a.m. gets the same attention as one at noon. We take the address, the community name and a description of the trouble, then send a crew. Typical jobs include garbage disposal replacement, failed tanks, and sewer stoppages.

Where the pipes go

Sewer plants, authority service and septic homes

The Highlands report lists the Ajax Treatment Plant in the southeast, service through the Musconetcong Sewerage Authority, and two small plants, including one in Ledgewood. Septic tanks or cesspools serve the remaining areas. The township's Sewer Department also rehabilitates manholes and mains to reduce stormwater getting into the sanitary system.

Knowing which side your house is on changes the plan for a backup. A sewered house may have a blocked lateral, while a septic home may have a full tank. We say plainly when a problem is a septic matter, and we point you to a septic company, because our work covers the plumbing. For sewer lines, start with sewer line cleaning.

While we are on our way to Roxbury

Four steps worth doing first.

1Identify your water supplier

Look at the last bill. It says whether you use American Water or the township, and which number to call about the street.

2Close the main valve

Shut it if you can reach it safely. Leave it alone if it is corroded or stuck.

3Protect the floor and contents

Move valuables up. Put buckets under drips and towels around a toilet base.

4Gas smell? Outside first

Walk out, get away from the building, then dial 911 or the gas utility. Plumbing comes after.

Lakes

Lakes and brook corridors

Highlands figures list 173 acres of Lake Hopatcong inside the township, along with Lake Musconetcong, Chesler Lake and Horseshoe Lake. The county plan counts 54 people and 35 buildings in the 100-year flood areas and 59 flood policies. Waterfront and low-lying houses should consider a battery backup pump. Check valves on drains can jam open with debris, so ask the construction office whether they are allowed before buying one.

Older houses

Housing built before 1980

About 65 percent of Roxbury's housing units went up before 1980, and nearly half before 1970, according to ACS tabulations. That says nothing about any single house, but it does mean older fixtures and valves are likely somewhere in town. When we visit, we look at the shutoffs and supply lines near the problem, and explain whether a repiping conversation is worth having.
