Burst pipe repair means stopping the water, cutting out the cracked or split section of pipe and joining in sound pipe so the line holds pressure again. In Randolph, NJ the call usually comes from a basement spray, a soaked ceiling or a frozen line that finally gave way.

Every minute a pressurized pipe runs, it can put gallons into insulation, drywall and flooring. The steps below slow the damage until a plumber is at the door.

A pipe just burst: your first five minutes

Do these in order. The goal is less water, no shock risk and a clear path for the plumber.

1Shut the water off

Close the main valve where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops, then open a low faucet to drain the pipes.

2Cut the power if water is near it

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off that area at the breaker only if the breaker is dry and reachable.

3Contain and clear

Move furniture and valuables away, put buckets under drips and clear a walkway to the shut-off and the damaged spot.

4Call and describe

Tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from, whether the main is off and whether anyone smells gas or sees sparks.

Repair options

Cracked or broken pipe: how we repair it

The right fix depends on how long the damaged run is and how much of the pipe around it is worn. A single split in good pipe is a short repair. A line that has failed in two or three places is telling you something about the whole run.

Winter damage

Frozen pipe repair and how to avoid a second freeze

Water expands as it freezes, and the ice plug is often not what splits the pipe. The split usually forms in the stretch between the ice and a closed faucet, where pressure builds. You may not see a leak until the ice melts, so a frozen line that thaws on its own can start flooding without warning.

If a pipe is frozen but has not burst, open the nearest faucet, then warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer or warm towels, working from the faucet side toward the freeze. Never use a torch or open flame. If you see a crack, bulge or frost on a split, shut the main and call.

To prevent a repeat, insulate pipes in garages, exterior walls and crawlspaces, and keep heat running in rooms that have supply lines. A plumber can also reroute an exposed line away from the cold side of the house.

Know your shut-offs

Who owns the pipe, and where the water stops

Randolph owners are responsible for the water service from the curb shut-off to the house. A break on that stretch is yours to repair. Past the house wall, everything is yours as well, so the question is only which section failed.

The township's Division of Water and Sewer handles the system side, and after hours water emergencies route through police dispatch. If your neighbors lose pressure too, or a boil water notice is posted, the cause may be the street main rather than your pipe. That has happened here, when a main break on April 5, 2024 led to a boil water advisory. Check that first, but if water is in your house, call us anyway.

By pipe type

Copper, drain and supply pipe failures

Copper pipe repair

Copper can split from freezing, pit from corrosion or fail at old soldered joints. We cut back to clean pipe and make new joints.

Drain pipe repair

A cracked drain leaks only when water runs through it, so the stain appears below a bathroom or kitchen. The damaged section is cut out and replaced.

Pipe leak repair

Pinholes and weeping joints are the early stage. A small repair now can prevent the burst later.

Hidden supply lines

A leak you cannot see needs locating first. Our leak detection work pinpoints it before any wall is opened.

Older houses

Why one burst often leads to a bigger look at the plumbing

About 54 percent of Randolph's homes, or 5,036 of 9,356 units, were built before 1980, according to the township's housing data. We do not assume what any house is piped with, and age alone proves nothing. But after a burst, we check the nearby pipe and joints so that the next failure is not a few feet from this one.

If the inspection shows widespread wear, we will explain when repiping makes more sense than another patch, and what that job involves.

Not every leak is a burst

When a pinhole leak can wait for morning

A slow drip at a fitting that you can catch with a bowl is not the same as a burst. If you can close the shut-off valve to that fixture and the rest of the house still has water, you can usually book a same-day appointment when available instead of an emergency visit.

Call right away if water is coming through a ceiling, spraying under pressure, pooling near electrical equipment or soaking a finished floor. Those cannot wait, and they escalate quickly.

At the street

Is the break in your pipe or the line from the curb?

A wet yard, a sinking patch of lawn or a meter that spins with the house dry can mean the service line failed. We handle that work through our water service repair crews, and a call to Denville or other nearby towns follows the same process.
